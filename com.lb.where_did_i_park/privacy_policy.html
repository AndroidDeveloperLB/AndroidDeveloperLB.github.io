Privacy Policy for Where did I park?

Effective Date: August 25, 2026

The sole developer of this App, Where did I park?, is the Data Controller responsible for the processing of your personal data. The developer does not have their own servers and does not directly collect, store, or process any personal data from you. All data processing is done locally on your device or through third-party services as described below.

Privacy Policy Summary

This privacy policy explains how your data is handled when you use the app, Where did I park?. Crucially, while the app requires access to your Location Data to function, this data is stored completely locally on your device and is never uploaded to external servers or shared by the developer.

The app itself does not directly collect or store your personal data on external servers. External data processing is strictly limited to third-party services from Google used to provide features such as advertising (AdMob), anonymous usage statistics and crash reports (Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics), and app configuration (Firebase Remote Config).

Since the app developer does not have access to any personal data, it is not possible to fulfill requests for data access or deletion. For any data-related concerns regarding third parties, you should contact the third-party providers directly.

 

Location Data Handling

Because the core purpose of this App is to help you find your parked vehicle, it requires access to your device's Location Data. We want to be completely transparent about how this data is accessed, collected, and used:

  • Access and Collection: The App requests access to your precise location (both in the foreground and the background). Background location access is required to automatically detect parking events (such as disconnecting from a Bluetooth device or utilizing activity recognition) and save your spot even when the App is closed or not actively in use.
  • Use of Data: Your location data is used strictly to save the coordinates of your parked vehicle and to provide you with navigation directions back to that spot.
  • Storage and Sharing: Your location data is stored solely locally on your device. The App does not transmit, upload, or share your location logs with the developer, external servers, or third parties. Any routing or map display utilizes standard on-device integrations (such as Google Play Services and Google Maps).

User Rights and Data Removal

Because I, as the app developer, do not directly store or process any of your personal data or location data on external servers, I have no way to identify you or to link your identity to any data collected by the third-party services (AdMob, Firebase Analytics, Crashlytics, Remote Config). Therefore, I am not able to fulfill requests for data access, rectification, or erasure from these services.

These third-party services are responsible for their own data handling and offer their own mechanisms for data removal or consent management. For any data-related requests, you will need to contact the respective third-party provider directly as per their privacy policies. To clear your saved parking data, you can delete the logs directly within the App or clear the App's data via your device settings.
